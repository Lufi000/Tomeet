## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I import books?

On the Library screen, tap the “+” button in the top-right corner and select a local EPUB file.

### Where are AI conversations stored?

AI conversation records are stored locally on your device by default. If iCloud sync is enabled, they are synchronized across devices tied to your Apple ID.

### How can I delete my data?

You can long-press a book in the library to delete it, or clear all conversation history and notes from the Profile screen. To fully delete account-related data, please contact us.

### What if the app crashes or won’t open?

Please try the following:

1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
2. Restart your iPhone.
3. Make sure you have the latest versions of iOS and Tomeet installed.
4. If the issue persists, email us with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the problem.
