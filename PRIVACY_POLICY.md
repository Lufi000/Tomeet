# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**最后更新 / Last Updated:** 2026-08-25

## 引言 / Introduction

Tomeet（以下简称"我们"或"本应用"）是一款 AI 阅读应用，致力于让你与书籍随时对话，并将阅读转化为个人理解。我们非常重视你的隐私保护。本隐私政策说明了我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护你的个人信息。

Tomeet (“we”, “us”, or “the App") is an AI reading app designed to let you converse with books and turn reading into personal understanding. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

## 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

### 你提供的信息 / Information You Provide

- **书籍内容**：你导入或添加到书架的电子书（EPUB 等）及其元数据。
- **阅读数据**：阅读进度、书签、高亮、笔记。
- **AI 对话记录**：你向书籍提出的问题、追问以及 AI 回复内容。
- **账户信息**：如果你使用 iCloud 同步，我们可能会收集由 Apple 提供的匿名同步令牌。

- **Book content**: eBooks (EPUB, etc.) you import or add to your library and their metadata.
- **Reading data**: reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, and notes.
- **AI conversation records**: questions you ask the book, follow-ups, and AI responses.
- **Account information**: if you use iCloud sync, we may collect anonymous sync tokens provided by Apple.

### 自动收集的信息 / Information Collected Automatically

- **设备信息**：设备型号、iOS 版本、应用版本、唯一设备标识符（用于崩溃分析）。
- **使用数据**：功能使用频率、页面访问情况，用于改进产品体验。
- **诊断信息**：崩溃日志和性能数据，仅用于排查技术问题。

- **Device information**: device model, iOS version, app version, and unique device identifiers (for crash analytics).
- **Usage data**: feature usage frequency and screen visits, used to improve the product experience.
- **Diagnostic information**: crash logs and performance data, used solely for technical troubleshooting.

## 我们如何使用信息 / How We Use Your Information

- 提供、维护和改进本应用的核心阅读与 AI 对话功能。
- 同步你的书架、阅读进度、笔记和对话记录 across 你的设备。
- 分析使用趋势以优化用户体验。
- 排查错误和技术问题。

- Provide, maintain, and improve the core reading and AI conversation features.
- Sync your library, reading progress, notes, and conversation history across your devices.
- Analyze usage trends to optimize user experience.
- Troubleshoot errors and technical issues.

## AI 服务与第三方 / AI Services and Third Parties

本应用使用第三方 AI 服务（如 OpenAI、Anthropic 等）来处理你的自然语言问题。上传到 AI 服务的内容仅用于生成回复，**不会被用于训练第三方 AI 模型**。

We use third-party AI services (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) to process your natural-language questions. Content sent to AI services is used solely to generate responses and **will not be used to train third-party AI models**.

## 数据存储与安全 / Data Storage and Security

- 你的书籍、笔记、高亮和对话记录默认存储在你的设备本地。
- 如果你开启 iCloud 同步，数据会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 服务在设备间同步。
- 我们采用行业标准的安全措施保护你的数据，但请注意，没有任何网络传输或电子存储方式能够保证 100% 安全。

- Your books, notes, highlights, and conversation records are stored locally on your device by default.
- If you enable iCloud sync, data is synchronized across devices via Apple’s iCloud service.
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 你的权利 / Your Rights

你可以随时：
- 访问、修改或删除你的笔记和高亮。
- 删除单条 AI 对话或清空全部对话历史。
- 通过 iOS 系统设置管理 iCloud 同步。
- 联系我们请求导出或删除你的个人数据。

You may at any time:
- Access, modify, or delete your notes and highlights.
- Delete individual AI conversations or clear all conversation history.
- Manage iCloud sync through iOS system settings.
- Contact us to request export or deletion of your personal data.

## 儿童隐私 / Children’s Privacy

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。如果你发现 13 岁以下儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们将采取措施删除相关信息。

The App is not intended for children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.
