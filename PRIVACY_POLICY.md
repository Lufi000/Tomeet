# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-18

## Introduction

Tomeet (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) is an AI reading app designed to help you talk to books and turn reading into personal understanding. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Book content**: eBooks (EPUB, etc.) and their metadata that you import or add to your library.
- **Reading data**: reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, and notes.
- **AI conversation records**: questions you ask the book, follow-up questions, and AI responses.
- **Account information**: if you enable iCloud sync, we may collect anonymous sync tokens provided by Apple.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device information**: device model, iOS version, app version, and unique device identifiers used solely for crash analytics.
- **Usage data**: feature usage frequency and screen visits, used to improve the product experience.
- **Diagnostic information**: crash logs and performance data, used only for technical troubleshooting.

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide, maintain, and improve the core reading and AI conversation features.
- Sync your library, reading progress, notes, and conversation history across your devices.
- Analyze usage trends to optimize user experience.
- Troubleshoot errors and technical issues.

## AI Services and Third Parties

The App's AI conversation feature is powered by a third-party large language model, **DeepSeek** (operated by DeepSeek, api.deepseek.com). No AI model runs inside the App itself; all AI processing happens server-side.

Requests are sent from the App to a lightweight backend proxy operated by us, which forwards them to DeepSeek over an encrypted connection. The App never communicates with the AI provider directly, and no third-party API key is embedded in the App.

Content transmitted to the AI provider is limited to what is needed to answer your question: the text of your messages and relevant book context (book title, author, and short excerpts of the book you are discussing). The App has no account system, and we do not transmit your name, email address, device identifiers, or other personal information to AI providers.

Content sent to AI services is used solely to generate responses and is handled in accordance with the AI provider's privacy and security standards. **We do not allow third-party AI providers to use your content for training their models.**

Apple's iCloud is used only if you enable sync, for synchronizing your library and data across your own devices.

## Data Storage and Security

- Your books, notes, highlights, and conversation records are stored locally on your device by default.
- If you enable iCloud sync, data is synchronized across devices via Apple’s iCloud service.
- We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## Your Rights

You may at any time:

- Access, modify, or delete your notes and highlights.
- Delete individual AI conversations or clear all conversation history.
- Manage iCloud sync through iOS system settings.
- Contact us to request export or deletion of your personal data.

## Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.
